Why The Attic Is Your Home's Largest Thermal Vulnerability

In standard residential architecture, hot air naturally rises toward the top of the house via convection, creating a pressure dynamic known as the stack effect. During winter months, warm conditioned air searches for every electrical penetration, duct chase, and uninsulated ceiling drywall seam, escaping directly into the cold attic.

During summer, the dynamic reverses: an under-insulated attic space can reach temperatures exceeding 140°F (60°C), radiating intense heat down through your ceilings into bedrooms and forcing air conditioning units to run continuously.

Our 5-Stage Attic Engineering Methodology

Simply adding fresh insulation over an existing, leaky attic fails to fix air infiltration. We follow an engineered protocol:

1
Diagnostic Inspection & Attic Hatch Sealing

We measure existing insulation depth, check for roof moisture leaks, and install weatherstripped, insulated attic tent covers over access staircases.

2
Penetration & Top-Plate Air Sealing

Using one-part expanding polyurethane foam, we seal every interior top plate wall seam, wire penetration, plumbing stack, and recessed ceiling canister.

3
Soffit Ventilation Baffle Installation

We install durable rafter baffles (accu-vents) at every rafter bay to guarantee unobstructed continuous airflow from soffit intake vents to ridge vents.

4
Calibrated Blown-In Application

Our mobile blowing rigs evenly distribute loose-fill fiberglass or borate-treated cellulose to exact R-49 or R-60 ruler markers installed across the attic floor.

5
Infrared Thermal Audit & R-Value Certificate

We capture post-installation infrared scans to certify 100% void-free coverage and issue an official certificate for your federal tax rebate documentation.

Service Specific FAQs

If your existing insulation is dry, mold-free, and undamaged by rodents, it does not need to be removed. We can air-seal key bypasses and blow the new high-performance insulation directly on top. If old insulation has water damage or rodent droppings, our HEPA vacuum extraction crew will safely remove and dispose of it first.

Modern virgin loose-fill fiberglass has negligible settling (less than 1–2%). Cellulose settles slightly (approximately 10–15% over its initial months), which is why our installers calculate and blow the "initial installed thickness" to guarantee the specified permanent settled R-value.
