1. Introduction & Scope

ThermaGuard™ Building Envelope & Energy Solutions Inc. ("ThermaGuard," "we," "us," or "our") operates this website and provides insulation installation, air sealing, crawl space encapsulation, and home thermal imaging assessment services. We are dedicated to safeguarding the privacy and security of your personal data and building performance records.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, store, and protect your information when you visit our website, utilize our thermal savings calculator, schedule an on-site energy evaluation, or contract our certified installation crew.

Our Promise: ThermaGuard does not sell, rent, or monetize your personal or residential diagnostic data to third-party marketing firms or data brokers.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on your interaction with ThermaGuard, we collect several categories of information required to deliver precision thermal engineering services:

  • Contact Identification Data: Full name, residential or commercial service address, email address, phone number, and preferred appointment timeframes.
  • Building Envelope Diagnostic Data: Year of construction, square footage, attic ceiling height, HVAC configuration, baseline utility bills, and existing insulation types (e.g., fiberglass, cellulose, rockwool).
  • Thermal Inspection & Audit Records: High-resolution FLIR infrared thermography scans, calibrated blower door airtightness readings (CFM50 metrics), rim joist inspection notes, and attic moisture readings.
  • Tax Credit & Rebate Documentation: Information necessary to issue Section 25C Energy Efficient Home Improvement Credit manufacturer certifications and file utility rebate submissions (e.g., ComEd, Nicor, Ameren).
  • Technical & Usage Telemetry: IP addresses, browser types, device specifications, referring URLs, and website interactions gathered via secure analytics cookies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational and engineering purposes, including:

  • Generating customized insulation proposals, R-value calculations, and thermal leak assessments.
  • Scheduling certified field technicians for blower door testing and attic air sealing installations.
  • Administering state and federal clean energy utility rebates and compiling compliance paperwork.
  • Providing lifetime customer support, manufacturer warranty registrations, and post-installation audit certificates.
  • Maintaining website performance, preventing security fraud, and enhancing user experience across desktop and mobile devices.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We maintain strict confidentiality protocols. We disclose information only to vetted third parties under strict confidentiality and non-disclosure obligations:

  • Energy Efficiency Program Sponsors: State utility rebate administrators (such as ComEd and Peoples Gas) when you authorize rebate deductions from your service invoice.
  • Material Manufacturers: Premium insulation suppliers (e.g., Owens Corning, BASF, Johns Manville) solely for verifying material batch warranty registrations.
  • Financing Partners: Certified home improvement financing providers if you elect to apply for monthly payment programs through our website.
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities: Only when strictly required by enforceable subpoena, court order, or applicable municipal building safety regulations.

5. Data Security & Storage Architecture

ThermaGuard employs multi-layered administrative, physical, and technological safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of customer records.

All digital communications between your browser and our servers are encrypted via TLS 1.3 encryption. Thermal audit imagery and diagnostic data are stored in SOC 2 Type II compliant cloud repositories with strict role-based access control limited to licensed energy auditors and project managers.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website utilizes first-party and essential performance cookies to preserve user preferences (including your dark/light theme setting and layout direction) and measure page responsiveness. You can adjust your browser settings to decline non-essential cookies at any time without impacting your ability to request an assessment.

7. Your Legal Rights & Privacy Choices

Regardless of your location, ThermaGuard respects your right to control your personal data. You are entitled to:

  • Access & Portability: Request an itemized copy of all building diagnostic reports, blower door certifications, and personal data in our possession.
  • Correction: Promptly update or rectify any outdated contact or structural building details.
  • Erasure: Request the deletion of non-essential records, subject to regulatory warranty and tax record retention laws.
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from non-service informational emails with a single click.
