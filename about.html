Our Founding Story

Founded on Building Science, Not Sales Gimmicks

ThermaGuard was founded in 2011 by a team of mechanical engineers and building performance analysts who grew frustrated watching homeowners spend tens of thousands of dollars on oversized furnace replacements while warm air leaked unobstructed right into their attics.

We built ThermaGuard around a singular principle: treat the building as a complete dynamic system. By combining calibrated blower door testing, infrared thermography, and precision material application, we help property owners stop heat loss at its physical source.
