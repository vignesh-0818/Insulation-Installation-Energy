2. Energy Audits & Pre-Installation Assessments

ThermaGuard provides comprehensive diagnostic building science assessments, including FLIR infrared thermography, calibrated blower door envelope leakage testing (CFM50), attic depth probes, and combustion safety checks.

  • Estimates vs. Final Proposals: Figures generated through our online Savings Calculator or preliminary telephone consultations are informational estimates based on standard regional building averages. Binding project scopes require on-site diagnostic verification.
  • Diagnostic Accuracy: Customers agree to provide accurate building history data, including unpermitted additions, known roof leaks, attic ventilation modifications, and previous chemical pest treatments.

3. Pricing, Invoicing, Tax Credits & Utility Rebates

All quotes clearly delineate material specifications, labor, baffle installations, air sealing passes, and anticipated utility incentives.

  • Payment Milestones: Standard contracts require a 25% scheduling deposit upon contract execution, with the remaining 75% balance due upon project completion and passing of post-installation blower door verification.
  • Inflation Reduction Act (Section 25C): ThermaGuard provides an official Manufacturer Certification Statement and line-item invoice detailing eligible insulation and air sealing material costs for Section 25C Energy Efficient Home Improvement tax credits (up to $1,200 annually). ThermaGuard does not provide tax advice; eligibility should be confirmed with your tax advisor.
  • Utility Rebate Pass-Through: Where eligible under regional utility programs (e.g., ComEd, Nicor, Ameren), instant point-of-sale discounts are contingent on property owner utility account qualification and documentation.

4. Site Preparation & Property Access

Safe and timely installation requires customer cooperation regarding workspace access and safety protocols:

  • Attic & Crawl Space Clearance: Property owners must ensure clear, unobstructed pathway access to attic access hatches or crawl space entrances. Personal items within 6 feet of access portals should be relocated prior to crew arrival.
  • Spray Foam Cure Window: Two-component polyurethane spray foam applications require strict compliance with manufacturer and OSHA/EPA safety protocols. Occupants and pets must vacate the premises during application and remain off-site for a mandatory 24-hour ventilation and curing window.
  • Utilities: Homeowner agrees to provide continuous 110V/220V electrical supply and functional water access required for equipment operation.

5. Hazardous Conditions & Unforeseen Structural Defects

ThermaGuard prioritizes occupant health and building safety. If our field technicians encounter any of the following pre-existing hazards, work will be paused until certified remediation is performed:

  • Active active knob-and-tube electrical wiring in contact with insulation bays.
  • Friable asbestos-containing materials (ACM) or loose vermiculite insulation (e.g., Zonolite).
  • Active roof leaks, structural rotted rafters, or severe black mold infesting ceiling sheathing.
  • Severe rodent or bat infestations posing biohazard risks.
Remediation Scope: ThermaGuard is not a hazardous abatement contractor. If abatement is required, an amendment will outline rescheduled timeline parameters without forfeiture of customer deposits.

6. Warranties & 10-Year Workmanship Guarantee

We stand behind our building science installations with industry-leading guarantees:

  • 10-Year Air-Seal & Workmanship Warranty: ThermaGuard guarantees that all air sealing foams, acoustic caulks, and insulation barriers will remain structurally intact without sagging, settling below specified R-value minimums, or pulling away from framing members for 10 full years from the date of completion.
  • Manufacturer Material Warranties: Insulation products (blown fiberglass, dense-pack cellulose, and closed-cell polyurethane) carry lifetime or multi-decade manufacturer warranties against shrinkage and decay.
  • Exclusions: Warranties do not cover failures resulting from structural roof collapse, unmitigated foundation moisture, water pipe bursts, acts of God, or unauthorized modifications made by third-party trades.

7. Cancellations, Rescheduling & Change Orders

Appointments may be rescheduled up to 48 hours prior to the scheduled installation date without penalty. Cancellations made with less than 48 hours notice may be subject to a nominal materials restocking and dispatch fee.

Any changes to the agreed scope of work (such as adding knee-wall insulation or recessed lighting fire-rated covers) will be formally executed via written Change Order specifying adjusted costs before execution.

8. Standards, Compliance & Dispute Resolution

All installations conform strictly with the Building Performance Institute (BPI) Gold Contractor standards, the International Energy Conservation Code (IECC), and local Illinois municipal building codes.

These Terms are governed by the laws of the State of Illinois. In the event of any dispute, the parties agree to seek resolution through good-faith mediation prior to filing any formal legal claims in Cook County, Illinois.
