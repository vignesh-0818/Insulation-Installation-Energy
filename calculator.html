Calculation Methodology & Documented Assumptions

1. Heating & Cooling Baseline Fraction

According to the U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Residential Energy Consumption Survey (RECS), space heating and air conditioning comprise approximately 52% of total residential utility expenditures, with base electrical loads (refrigeration, lighting, electronics) making up the remaining 48%.

2. Air Sealing Synergy Multiplier

Insulation alone resists conduction but does not stop convection. Incorporating whole-home air sealing (Level 2 & Level 3) yields higher total load reductions by preventing the stack effect from bypassing the thermal blanket.

3. Long-Term Utility Escalation Model

Cumulative 5-year and 10-year projections factor in a modest 3.0% compound annual escalation in regional electric and natural gas tariff rates, reflecting long-term historical utility trends.

4. Disclaimer & Guarantee Boundary

All calculated figures are provided strictly for educational modeling and estimating purposes. They are not guaranteed warranties of specific utility bill amounts, as individual household occupant behavior, thermostat setpoints, and seasonal degree-day anomalies vary.
