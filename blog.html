The True Cause of Destructive Roof Ice Dams (And How to Fix Them Forever)

Roof ice dams occur when indoor warmth leaks through attic floors and warms upper roof sheathing above freezing temperature. The resulting snowmelt runs down to sub-freezing eaves, refreezing into thick ice ridges that back water up beneath roof shingles. Quick Action Guide

Practical Tips for Home Energy Efficiency

Small thermal improvements and routine envelope inspections can significantly reduce HVAC workload and enhance daily indoor comfort.

Insulate Attic Hatches

Uninsulated attic drop-down stairs and pull-down hatches allow substantial warm air leakage. Install a rigid foam insulation box with durable perimeter weatherstripping.

Air-Seal Rim Joists

Basement and crawl space rim joists are notorious for ground-level cold drafts. Applying closed-cell spray foam seals framing junctions against moisture and outdoor infiltration.

Maintain Soffit Airflow

Ensure insulation baffles (vent chutes) keep attic eave soffits clear of loose fill. Unhindered soffit-to-ridge airflow prevents winter moisture accumulation and roof ice dams.

Schedule Thermal Audits

A professional diagnostic assessment with calibrated blower door depressurization and infrared imaging pinpoints hidden air leaks before seasonal extremes arrive.
